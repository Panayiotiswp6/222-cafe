## Subtle, grounded reasons to choose 222

- A choice of settings: cosy upholstered booths, street-facing window tables, and terrace seating. These features are directly visible in the real venue photographs. Avoid claims that they are unique to Athens or unavailable at other cafes.
- Coffee and a proper bite in one stop: freddo/Greek coffee, panini, pizza and desserts are on the merchant's own current Wolt menu: https://wolt.com/en/grc/athens/restaurant/222-espresso-bar .
- An everyday neighbourhood meeting point on Papagou 140, Zografou; address confirmed by Wolt and NightHub.
