# 222 venue imagery — cinematic treatment

The owner requested a cinematic, photoreal, dimensional treatment of the café's existing space photographs, matching the dramatic quality of the coffee campaign. Their unrelated café examples were visual inspiration for lighting and image quality, not source architecture to copy.

The image generation tool edited each actual local venue photograph separately. The brief retains the recognisable 222 room, wood ceiling and ductwork, paper pendant lamps, upholstered booths, characteristic chairs, tables, window frontage, greenery and Papagou street context while improving warmth, detail, depth and exposure. Small temporary clutter may be cleaned up. These are creative visual interpretations, not unedited photographs or a measured reconstruction; minor generated details may differ.
