## Menu scope

Wolt's current merchant listing includes espresso and freddo coffee, teas, chocolate drinks, juices, panini, pizza, cereal bars, milkshakes, butter croissant, waffles, cheesecake, banoffee, profiterole, homemade lemonade and sour cherry drink, soft drinks, and bottled beer. Do not invent a cocktail selection. Product prices shown by Wolt are delivery prices and should not be represented as verified dine-in prices.

## Hours

Restaurant Guru and LaptopFriendly agree on Monday–Friday 07:30–23:00 and Saturday–Sunday 08:30–23:00. Nighthub has inconsistent 11:30 closing values. These are third-party sources; prefer a phone CTA or owner confirmation before publishing exact venue hours. Wolt's times are explicitly delivery times (not venue operating hours).
